A hearty one-pot Cajun-style dish with smoky sausage, red beans, and brown rice. The base is fully vegetarian — sausage is browned separately and added at the table so everyone can serve themselves.

## Ingredients
- 4 cups brown rice
- 4 (15 oz) cans kidney beans, drained and rinsed
- 2.5 pounds smoked sausage (such as andouille), sliced into rounds
- 3 large bell peppers, diced
- 2 large onions, diced
- 7 garlic cloves, minced
- 8 cups vegetable broth, for cooking the rice
- 2 cups vegetable broth, for the simmer
- 1.5 teaspoons dried thyme
- 1.5 teaspoons dried oregano
- 2 tablespoons paprika
- 1 teaspoons cayenne pepper
- 1.5 tablespoons chili powder
- 1.5 teaspoons garlic powder
- 1.5 teaspoons onion powder
- 2 teaspoons salt
- 1 tablespoons olive oil, for browning the sausage
- 1 tablespoons olive oil, for sautéing the vegetables

## Steps
1. **Cook the Rice:** In a medium saucepan, bring 8 cups vegetable broth, for cooking the rice to a boil. Stir in 4 cups brown rice, reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer until tender and the liquid is absorbed.
2. **Brown the Sausage (Separately):** In a separate skillet, heat 1 tablespoons olive oil, for browning the sausage over medium-high heat. Add 2.5 pounds smoked sausage (such as andouille), sliced into rounds and cook, stirring occasionally, until browned and cooked through, about 8 minutes. Set aside off heat — this stays in its own pan so it can be added individually at serving.
3. **Sauté the Veggies:** In a large pot, heat 1 tablespoons olive oil, for sautéing the vegetables over medium heat. Add 3 large bell peppers, diced, 2 large onions, diced, and 7 garlic cloves, minced and sauté until softened and fragrant, about 6-7 minutes.
4. **Combine and Simmer:** Stir in 4 (15 oz) cans kidney beans, drained and rinsed, 2 cups vegetable broth, for the simmer, 1.5 teaspoons dried thyme, 1.5 teaspoons dried oregano, 2 tablespoons paprika, 1 teaspoons cayenne pepper, 1.5 tablespoons chili powder, 1.5 teaspoons garlic powder, 1.5 teaspoons onion powder, and 2 teaspoons salt. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered, stirring occasionally, to let the flavors meld.
5. **Fold in the Rice and Serve:** Fold the cooked 4 cups brown rice into the pot and stir to combine. Simmer together a few minutes longer, then taste and adjust salt and cayenne as needed. Serve the rice and bean mixture in bowls, and set out the browned 2.5 pounds smoked sausage (such as andouille), sliced into rounds on the side for anyone who wants to stir it in.

## Notes
Keeping the sausage in its own pan (with its own oil) means the main pot never touches meat, so it's genuinely vegetarian-safe, not just meat-light. Use a separate serving spoon for the sausage at the table to avoid cross-contact. The beans and rice together already provide solid protein for the vegetarian portions. Cayenne is medium heat — adjust up or down to taste.
