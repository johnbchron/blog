## Ingredients

### Dough
- 2 yeast pizza dough balls (18–20 oz / 500–570 g each)
- 2 tbsp cornmeal or semolina, for dusting

### Sauce
- 1½ cups jarred spaghetti sauce
- 1–2 tbsp tomato paste (optional, to thicken)
- Pinch of dried oregano or red pepper flakes (optional)

### Toppings
- 16 oz low-moisture mozzarella, shredded
- ½ cup grated Parmesan
- 4 oz pepperoni slices (or toppings of choice)
- About 8 fresh basil leaves, to finish
- Olive oil, for drizzling

## Instructions

1. **Preheat the oven.** Place a pizza stone, steel, or upside-down sheet pan on the lowest rack and preheat to 500°F (or as hot as your oven goes). Let it heat for a full 45 minutes.
2. **Prep the sauce.** If the spaghetti sauce is runny, simmer it in a pan for 10–15 minutes until it holds a line when you drag a spoon through it, then let it cool. Or stir in the tomato paste to thicken it without cooking. Blend or mash chunky sauce so it spreads evenly. Taste and add oregano or red pepper flakes if it seems flat.
3. **Stretch the first pizza.** Press one dough ball into a flat disk with your fingertips, leaving a 1-inch rim. Lift and stretch it over your knuckles, rotating, until it's 14–16 inches across. Lay it on a peel or the back of a sheet pan dusted with cornmeal.
4. **Top it.** Spread about ¾ cup of sauce thinly, leaving the rim bare. Scatter half the mozzarella, half the Parmesan, and half the pepperoni. Work quickly, and give the peel a shake to make sure the pizza slides.
5. **Bake.** Slide the pizza onto the hot stone or pan and bake 8–12 minutes, until the crust is deep golden and the cheese is bubbling with browned spots.
6. **Finish and repeat.** Top with torn basil and a drizzle of olive oil. Rest 2–3 minutes before slicing. Let the stone reheat about 5 minutes, then repeat with the second pizza.

## Notes

- Check the pizza at the 8-minute mark, since oven temperatures vary.
- Spaghetti sauce is thinner than pizza sauce, so keep the layer thin and pat the mozzarella dry to avoid a soggy center.
- No peel? Build the pizza on parchment and slide it all in. Pull the parchment out after 4 minutes for a crisper bottom.
- Swap the pepperoni for mushrooms, peppers, sausage, or olives. Keep wet toppings light.
